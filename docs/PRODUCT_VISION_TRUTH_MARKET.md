# Truth Market Product Vision
*Conversation Date: June 30, 2025*

## Core Product Insight
Transform from a multi-category Q&A system into a **consensus discovery engine for contentious truths**.

## The Revelation
"Will AI reach consciousness?" classified as prediction, not philosophical - this is perfect. The most interesting questions aren't about categories, they're about **uncertainty and consensus**.

## Product Focus: The Truth Market

### What You're Really Building
- Not a Q&A system
- A **consensus discovery engine for contentious truths**
- Value: Questions where experts disagree, future is uncertain, truth is contested

### The Simplification
Replace all routing (philosophical, knowledge, prediction, true/false) with ONE thing:
**A prediction market for truth**

Every query becomes: "What's the probability this is/will be true?"

## The "AIs as Traders" Concept

### How It Works
1. Each AI is a "trader" with:
   - Stake: Confidence level (0-100%)
   - Position: YES/NO/UNCERTAIN
   - Track Record: Historical accuracy

2. Market Mechanism:
   - Confidence weights influence consensus
   - Reputation scores from historical accuracy
   - Diversity bonus for disagreement

3. Example:
```
Claude: YES (85% confident)
GPT-4: YES (70% confident)
Gemini: NO (60% confident)
= 67% probability of YES
```

## Truth Arena 2.0: The Missing Piece

### Humans as Market Makers
Not just players, but:
1. **Market makers** (creating liquidity)
2. **Resolution oracles** (determining truth)
3. **Question curators** (surfacing what matters)

### The Game Loop
**Daily Truth Challenge:**
- See AI consensus on a prediction
- Make your own prediction
- Stake reputation points
- Track accuracy over time
- Compete on leaderboards

### Data Refinement Value
Every human interaction provides:
- Calibration data
- Expertise mapping
- Bias detection
- Resolution consensus for ground truth

### Product Evolution
**Phase 1**: Truth Arena game (pick best answer)
**Phase 2**: Prediction Arena (compete against AI consensus)
**Phase 3**: Truth Market (submit questions, stake reputation)

## Perfect Use Cases
Questions like "COVID leaked from lab":
- Highly contentious
- Cannot be definitively proven
- Different AIs have different biases
- 50% confidence, 75% agreement = honest uncertainty

## Your Moat
1. Multi-AI consensus (expensive to replicate)
2. Prediction tracking (builds trust)
3. Uncertainty quantification (unique value)
4. Controversial topics (where others fear)
5. Human-AI collaboration data

## The Vision
**One app. Two modes:**
1. **Play**: Make daily predictions, beat the AIs
2. **Judge**: Help resolve old predictions

You're building **the world's truth consensus engine** powered by human-AI collaboration.
